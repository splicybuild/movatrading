WHY A SECTOR IS MOVING

MOVA compares the daily change of representative stocks in each sector. Positive breadth means more constituents are advancing; negative breadth means weakness is broader. Individual stock notes use current price direction, daily momentum and the size of the move to explain what the market data is signalling. These are research signals rather than investment recommendations.
